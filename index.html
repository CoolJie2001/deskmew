DeskMew

A desktop cat that naps on your keyboard.
Windows 10 / 11 · Works fully offline · No data collected

Meet your new coworker

DeskMew is a playful black cat that lives on your desktop. As you type, it reaches out and taps the keys with its paws. Move your mouse and it turns its head to follow the cursor. Pet its head and it closes its eyes happily; ignore it too long and it falls asleep; annoy it and it thumps its tail on your desk.

Features

  • Live keyboard interaction — the cat taps along as you type, using the correct paw for each key
  • Mouse following — its head and eyes track your cursor all day long
  • Petting — click its head and it enjoys it (blushes included)
  • Typing sound effects — three key sound types, mutable from the context menu
  • Multiple skins — black cat, orange tabby, and skeletal-animation keyboard cats
  • Auto-start — optionally launches with Windows
  • 100% offline — no network requests, no accounts, no tracking

Privacy

DeskMew works entirely offline. It reads which physical key was pressed (never the typed content) and your mouse pointer position so the cat can react — nothing is recorded, stored on servers, or shared. The only file it writes is a local settings file on your device.
